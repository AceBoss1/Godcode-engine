The short version

This site uses no advertising cookies, no analytics trackers, and no third-party cookies. This page describes the small amount of storage we do use.

What we store

  • Your playground code. The playground may keep your most recent code in your browser’s local storage, so it is still there when you return. It never leaves your device unless you create a share link.
  • Security cookie. Our security provider, Cloudflare, may set a short-lived cookie to tell real visitors apart from automated attacks. It contains no personal information and cannot track you across websites.

What we do not do

  • No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party tracking.
  • No advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking.

Why there is no cookie banner

Cookie consent banners exist for sites that set optional or tracking cookies. We set none, so there is nothing to consent to. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first and a consent choice will be added.

Managing storage

You can clear stored data at any time through your browser settings (“clear site data”). Doing so will remove your saved playground code from that browser.
